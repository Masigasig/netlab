# Internet

### A global system of interconnected networks

The Internet is a vast collection of interconnected networks that allows devices around the world to communicate, share information, and access resources. These networks can belong to homes, schools, businesses, governments, and other organizations.

For example, you can use the Internet to access school resources even if you are not directly connected to your school’s local network. This is possible because the Internet connects networks across the globe.

![Internet](resource:assets/learning_material/images/chapter_0/Internet.gif)
*The Internet connects multiple networks together*

> Think of the Internet as a “network of networks”. It connects millions of private, public, academic, business, and government networks, enabling devices worldwide to communicate and share data.

### Key Points
* The Internet is not a single computer, but a global system of networks.

* Devices on the Internet can exchange information, such as emails, videos, files, and web pages.

* The Internet connects networks worldwide, not just within a local network.
