# Getting to Networking

### Understanding the fundamentals of computer networking

Computer Network is a system that connects different devices like computers, servers, printers 
so they can share resources and exhange information. This makes it possible to do things like 
sending emails, sharing files, or browse the internet.

So basically networking started with one computer wanting to share data with another. it can be 
simple as two computers connected with a cable.

![intro](resource:assets/images/content_image/network_fundamentals/intro.gif)
*Simple network: Two computers connected with a cable*

And it is possible to connect more devices and link multiple networks together with switches, 
routers, and other devices.

> &nbsp; &nbsp; At its core, networking is about "sharring and communcication". What began as two 
> computers exchanging data has now scaled into the global internet that connects 
> billions of devices.
